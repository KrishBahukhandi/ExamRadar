# Privacy Policy — ExamRadar India

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 2026  

Welcome to **ExamRadar India** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of Indian examination candidates and aspirants. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is secured, and your rights regarding your personal information.

---

## 1. Important Government Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

**ExamRadar India is an independent technological platform and educational tool. ExamRadar India is NOT an official representative of, affiliated with, associated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any government entity, including but not limited to the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS), State Public Service Commissions (PSCs), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), or any other Government body or Ministry.**

All examination dates, announcements, and recruitment notifications provided within the app are collected from publicly accessible official government websites. Every announcement card within the app provides the original official government source URL for independent candidate verification.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We strictly follow data minimization principles. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver personalized examination tracking and deadline reminders:

### A. Information You Provide
1. **Account Registration (Optional):** When creating an account to synchronize data across devices, we collect your **Email Address** and password (stored via cryptographic hashing by Supabase Auth).
2. **Profile & Exam Preferences (Optional):** You may choose to select target examination categories (e.g., Civil Services, SSC, Banking, Regulatory) and graduation status to receive tailored announcement alerts.
3. **Application Checklist Items:** Notes and completion checkmarks you record on your private application preparation checklist.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
1. **Device Push Token:** To send time-sensitive application deadline reminders and new recruitment alerts, we collect a unique device push token generated by **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**.
2. **Local Diagnostics:** Anonymous technical logs required to maintain application stability and detect crash events.

### C. Information We NEVER Collect
ExamRadar India **never** collects, requests, stores, or processes:
- Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, or government identity proofs.
- Candidate photographs, digital signatures, or scanned documents.
- Government portal login credentials or candidate passwords for external exam websites.
- Financial or payment card data, bank account numbers, or UPI IDs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Deliver push notifications for new recruitment notices and deadline reminders (7-day, 3-day, and 1-day alerts).
- Filter and display announcements tailored to your followed exams.
- Synchronize your application checklist progress across your devices.
- Maintain and enhance app security and prevent abuse.

We do **NOT** sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or marketing data brokers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure strictly to operate the application:
1. **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC):** Used for reliable dispatch of push notifications to your Android device. Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
2. **Supabase (Supabase Inc.):** Used for secure user authentication, token verification, and PostgreSQL database storage under strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

---

## 5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

- **Local Storage Security:** On your device, authentication session tokens are stored using AES-256 hardware-backed encryption via Android's `EncryptedSharedPreferences`.
- **In-Transit Encryption:** All communication between the app and our servers uses Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Data Retention:** Your data is retained only for as long as your account remains active.

---

## 6. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated records directly from within the app at any time:

1. Open **ExamRadar India**.
2. Navigate to **Account Settings > Delete Account**.
3. Confirm deletion.

Upon confirmation, our systems permanently purge your user profile, followed exams, checklist items, notification preferences, and auth credentials.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

ExamRadar India is intended for adult candidates, university graduates, and students preparing for competitive public examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical enhancements or regulatory changes. Significant changes will be announced within the app.
